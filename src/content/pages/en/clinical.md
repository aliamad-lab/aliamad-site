My clinical work centres on the care of severe, treatment-resistant and complex
psychiatric presentations, within the adult psychiatry department of Lille
University Hospital.

It follows a specialised, transdiagnostic approach at the interface between
clinical psychiatry and neuroscience, addressing situations characterised by
non-response to standard treatments, protracted course, or complex and
overlapping symptom presentations.

### Objective clinical psychiatry

Particular attention is given to developing an objective clinical psychiatry,
grounded in rigorous descriptive semiology, standardised assessment
instruments, and longitudinal analysis of clinical trajectories. This approach
is an essential prerequisite for the meaningful use of clinical neuroscience
methods and for individualising treatment strategies, notably within
translational research programmes such as [CALYPSO](/en/research#calypso).

### Treatment-resistant and complex presentations

My clinical work addresses the assessment and management of treatment-resistant
and clinically complex psychiatric disorders, including comorbid conditions,
psychiatric or otherwise. It falls more specifically within the field of mood
and psychotic disorders, considered in their severe, mixed or atypical forms,
notably through specialised programmes such as
[DEMHETER](https://demheter.fr/).

### Catatonia

Specific attention is given to the study of
[catatonia](https://www.catatonia.fr/), a psychomotor syndrome that is
frequently underdiagnosed, heterogeneous in presentation, and capable of
threatening functional or vital prognosis.

### Physical treatments

Finally, a central place is given to physical treatments — in particular
[electroconvulsive therapy](https://www.conn-ect.com/) and neuromodulation
techniques — understood as major therapeutic tools in severe or
treatment-resistant forms, and integrated into a broader clinical approach
aimed at better targeting treatments and better predicting the course of
psychiatric disorders.

## Clinical programmes and projects

- **[DEMHETER](https://demheter.fr/)** — assessment programme for mood
  disorders and their treatments.
- **[CALYPSO](https://calypso.univ-lille.fr/)** — research programme devoted to
  the objective measurement of psychiatric symptoms using computational methods.
- **[Catatonia](https://www.catatonia.fr/)** — resources on catatonic syndrome
  and the building of a clinical network.
- **[CONN-ECT](https://www.conn-ect.com/)** — a project to structure and connect
  specialised catatonia centres across France.
