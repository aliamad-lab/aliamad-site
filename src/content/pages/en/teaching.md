### University teaching and pedagogical approach

I am Professor of Psychiatry at the Faculty of Medicine of the University of
Lille, where I teach psychiatry at every level, from the undergraduate years
through to residency.

My teaching aims to convey a rigorous psychiatry, centred on clinical analysis,
on the care of severe, treatment-resistant and complex psychiatric disorders,
and on the development of treatment strategies.

My teaching covers primarily clinical psychiatry, the management of
treatment-resistant or complex presentations, and contemporary treatment
strategies, including both pharmacological and physical treatments.

### Structuring teaching, and psychiatric semiology

I am involved in structuring the teaching of psychiatry, particularly in the
field of psychiatric semiology.

To that end I take part in the work of the French Association for the Teaching
of Psychiatric Semiology ([AESP](http://www.asso-aesp.fr/)), which promotes a
rigorous, shared and up-to-date approach to clinical psychiatry, in the service
of both initial and specialist training.
