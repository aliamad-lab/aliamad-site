I am Professor of Psychiatry at the Faculty of Medicine of the University of
Lille and at Lille University Hospital.

My work brings together [clinical care](/en/clinical-activities) for severe and
treatment-resistant psychiatric disorders, [research](/en/research) in clinical
neuroscience, and university [teaching](/en/teaching).

My work focuses in particular on developing an objective clinical approach,
conceived as the necessary foundation for the neuroscientific interpretation of
psychiatric illness. I am also interested in the study of catatonia, and in the
mechanisms of action of physical treatments (electroconvulsive therapy,
neuromodulation).

Since February 2026 I have directed the [F2RSM Psy](https://www.f2rsmpsy.fr/),
the regional federation for research in psychiatry and mental health of the
Hauts-de-France region.
